Our story

There's no rushing this

SABR — صبر — is Arabic for patience, and it's the whole premise the brand is built on. Every piece is chosen with care rather than speed, so what reaches you is worth keeping.

The name and the practice both began in Cairo, and that same discipline now shapes every garment we make — quiet materials, honest cuts, nothing that needs a season to feel dated.
